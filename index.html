Robotics / Remote control

A small boat.
A meaningful purpose.

SALBA-BOT is a remote-controlled robotic boat designed for search-and-rescue operations in flooded and storm-affected areas.

Its project brief describes RF control, a camera, and a microphone for transmitting video and audio. “Salba” comes from the Filipino word for rescue.

02 / A little about me

An engineer's mindset.
A teacher's perspective.

I studied Electronics Engineering at the Polytechnic University of the Philippines, Maragondon. My experience spans higher education, quality assurance, data, and supply chain operations. Today, I teach at Lyceum of the Philippines — Cavite and work in supply chain operations at Gomaterials.

I enjoy exploring both sides of a system: how the physical pieces work, and how software can connect them. Away from the technical side, you'll find me playing Dota or NBA 2K.
